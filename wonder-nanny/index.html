Why

Babysitting keeps a child safe. I wanted to do more than that.

I have looked after the children of more than thirty-five families, photographed weddings on three continents, and spent long afternoons in museums with small people who asked better questions than I did. The hours parents are away do not have to be hours a child waits out in front of a screen. They can be the day the child draws the windmill, names the oldest painting in the room, or bakes the cookies you come home to.

Wonder Nanny is small by choice. I take the first bookings myself. The nannies who later join me shadow me first, sign the same promise, and are never alone with a new family on a first day. The work is rooted in my Catholic faith — every child is a wonder — and open to every family who wants their child looked after this way.

Every booking, the same rhythm

Six small rituals.

  1. Welcome. The Wonder bag is opened: a sketchbook, pencils, and today’s wonder question.
  2. Go. The activity, at the child’s pace. No rushing, no screens.
  3. Eat. A real meal or snack, organic where I can.
  4. Rest. Twenty quiet minutes: drawing, a story, a bench.
  5. One beautiful thing. The child names it. It goes on your card.
  6. Handover. The card, and ten photographs, in your inbox the same evening.

Who looks after your child

Me first. Then my circle.

For now, I take every booking myself. As Wonder Nanny grows, a small circle of nannies joins me — each with a certificate of conduct (VOG) for working with minors, paediatric first aid, two references I have spoken to, and a trial day at my side before any day alone. Every nanny signs the same promise:

I am present. I am patient. I bring a child only where I would bring my own. I tell the parents the truth about the day. I keep what I see in a home to myself.

Would you like to join? Wonder Nanny is rooted in Catholic faith and open to everyone who lives these values. Write to me.

The gift

Ten percent of every booking builds a home.

Ten percent of every Wonder Nanny booking goes to the Family of Pure Grace home for orphaned children in Bugiri, Uganda. It is a line on your invoice, not a footnote. Every quarter I publish what was transferred. And for every ten paid bookings, one Wonder Afternoon is given free to a single mother in my parish — the Wonder Gift.

Questions

The honest answers.

Is this registered childcare?

No. Wonder Nanny is a private service, not a gastouderbureau or kinderopvang in the national register, so you cannot claim childcare allowance for it. I hold a certificate of conduct and paediatric first aid, and I am insured for the care of minors.

Will my child’s photographs be published?

Never without your written consent, and never their face on social media. The ten photographs are yours. If you consent, I may show a sketchbook page or a card without names.

What if it rains?

Every outdoor itinerary has an indoor twin. We decide together the evening before.

Can I cancel?

Free until 48 hours before. Within 48 hours, half the package price, because the day was held for you. Illness of the child: free, always.

Do you take babies?

On request, one at a time, after we have spoken. The packages are written for ages three to twelve.

Who are you?

Wietske Hoencamp. Holistic health and lifestyle coach, photographer, ski instructor, studying psychology, Amsterdam. Wonder Nanny is a room in L’arte dell’amore.
