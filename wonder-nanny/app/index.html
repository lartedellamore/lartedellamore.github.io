Three scripts

The child refuses to leave

Kneel, name the feeling ("You want to stay. It is good here."), offer a last turn you count together, then a job for the walk ("You carry the map"). Never lift a child who says no unless unsafe.

The parent is late or changes the plan

Confirm by message what changes and when. Extra time is noted, not argued, and invoiced later. The child hears none of it.

An accident

Child safe first. Call 112 if in doubt. Then the parents, then Wietske. Write down the time and what happened while it is fresh. The truth, plainly, always.
